Legal & Operational Compliance

FARX Logistics — Terms & Conditions

1. Distance & Weight Slab Server Verification

All local delivery fares are calculated strictly on actual road distance (kilometers) and parcel weight (kilograms). Server-side validation locks coordinates and weight slabs. If actual physical or volumetric weight exceeds the declared weight at pickup, the differential slab charge must be settled prior to dispatch.

2. Packaging & Fragile Consignments

Senders are responsible for ensuring adequate internal cushioning and outer packaging suitable for road transport. Fragile items (glassware, electronics, liquids, cakes/perishables) must be explicitly declared prior to pickup.

3. Strictly Prohibited Items

FARX Logistics does not transport cash, bullion, jewelry, precious stones, narcotics, illegal substances, weapons, hazardous/flammable chemicals, or contraband. The sender holds complete legal liability for the contents inside sealed packages.

4. Transit Liability & Force Majeure

While FARX Logistics takes maximum care to ensure safe same-day and next-day delivery, transit schedules may be affected by severe weather, traffic blockades, strikes, or force majeure events. Maximum carrier liability for proven transit loss or damage of non-insured parcels is limited to standard courier liability norms.

5. Cancellation, Re-Attempt & Refund Policy

Cancellations requested before a rider is dispatched are eligible for a refund minus payment gateway processing charges. Once a rider has been dispatched to the pickup location, the base pickup fee is non-refundable. If the receiver is unavailable at the drop address, re-delivery or return-to-origin (RTO) charges apply.
