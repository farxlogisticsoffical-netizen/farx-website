    FARX Logistics terms & conditions

    1. Fares and parcel details

    Local delivery fares are based on the road distance between pickup and drop and on the parcel weight. Your total is shown before you pay. If the parcel handed over is heavier or larger than declared at booking, the fare will be revised and the difference must be paid before dispatch.

    2. Packaging and fragile items

    The sender is responsible for packing the parcel securely for road transport. Fragile items such as glassware, electronics, liquids, cakes and other perishables must be declared at booking. FARX is not responsible for damage caused by inadequate packaging.

    3. Prohibited items

    FARX does not carry cash, bullion, jewellery, precious stones, narcotics, illegal substances, weapons, hazardous or flammable chemicals, or contraband. The sender is fully legally responsible for what is inside a sealed package, and FARX may refuse or hold any parcel it believes breaks this rule.

    4. Timelines and liability

    We take great care to deliver on time, but timelines are estimates and may be affected by weather, traffic, strikes or other events beyond our control. For proven loss or damage of a parcel that was not insured, our liability is limited to standard courier liability norms.

    5. Proof of delivery

    A signed proof of delivery, or OTP confirmation at handover, is final evidence that the parcel was delivered. Transit claims are not accepted once delivery has been confirmed in this way.

    6. Cancellations and refunds

    A booking cancelled before a rider is dispatched is eligible for a refund, less payment gateway charges, to the original payment method. Once a rider has been dispatched to the pickup location, the base pickup fee is not refundable. If the receiver is unavailable at the drop address, re-delivery or return-to-origin charges apply.

    7. Claims

    If something goes wrong, contact our dispatch desk as soon as possible with your order number and photos of the parcel and its packaging, so that we can look into it quickly.

    8. Your information

    Names, phone numbers and addresses you give us are used only to complete your delivery and to provide support.

    9. Governing law

    These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any dispute is subject to the courts at Kozhikode, Kerala.
